## Steps
1. **Async authentication.** Add `Func<CancellationToken, ValueTask<Memory<byte>>>? AuthenticateAsync` to `ClientConfig`.
   - Keep the synchronous `Authenticate`.
   - Setting both is a configuration validation error.
   - It is called on every connect and reconnect, bounded by `HandshakeTimeout`.
2. **Plaintext warning.** Log once per client instance, on the first connect, at Warning level: `NexNet: unencrypted transport (<transport>); credentials and data are visible on the network.`
   - It applies to TCP (non-TLS), `ws://` and `http://` HttpSocket.
   - UDS is exempt (local). QUIC is always encrypted.
3. **`IsSecure`.** Add `INexusClient.IsSecure`:
   - true for TLS, WSS, HTTPS, QUIC and UDS;
   - false otherwise.

   Also add it to the client snapshot.
4. Add tests for each of the above, including log capture for the warning.
