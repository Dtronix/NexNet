## Steps
1. **Outbound calls:**
   - Invocation IDs.
   - A FIFO `CreditGate` with cancellable waits. Fire-and-forget calls also wait for credit: they are queued, never dropped.
   - Results.
   - Remote error detail → `NexusRemoteInvocationError{code, remoteType, remoteMessage}`.
   - Unauthorized → `NexusUnauthorizedError`.
2. **Cancellation:**
   - `{signal}` maps to `InvocationCancellation`.
   - A cancel before send rejects with `AbortError` (D17).
   - A cancel after send rejects immediately, and the late result is consumed.
   - Remove the abort listener on completion.
3. **Large messages.** Send and receive `MessageStart`/`Chunk`/`Abort` with budget reservation. Abort reasons map to `NexusBusyError`, `NexusMessageTooLargeError` and cancellation. Chunk writing interleaves with other frames.
4. **Inbound (server → client nexus) calls:**
   - Validate the method ID against `acceptedIds` (a ProtocolError otherwise).
   - Queue the call, and return the invocation credit **at dequeue for execution**.
   - Two concurrency limiters: the normal one (`maxConcurrentConnectionInvocations`) and the `SeparatePool` one.
   - Per-call `AbortSignal` cancellation.
   - Results, including streamed (large) results.
   - `remoteExceptionDetail` per the client config, and an `onInvocationException` hook.
5. **Auth.** `authenticate` may be sync or async, and is called on every connect.
6. **Counters.** Pending invocations, for the leak audit.

## Verify
The JS gate. Tests use the loopback transport and a scripted fake peer driven by golden frames.
