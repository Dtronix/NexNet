## Notes
- **Server-opened pipes** (the server opens a pipe to the client) exercise the client's registrant role. Make sure they are included.
- **Slow readers and bursts** (40 × 40 KiB, 4 MiB frames) use named in-host handlers: `pipe.readExact` and `channel.pump{count, size, delayMs}`.
- **Flow-control assertions** use host-measured timings and server-side window observations.
