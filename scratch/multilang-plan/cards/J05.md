## Steps
1. **Transport interface:** `{ write(Uint8Array[]): Promise<void>, onData(cb), onClose(cb), close(reason), isSecure, remoteCertificate }`.
2. **Framing.** Preamble `NnP\x14 000 01`, frame headers, and reassembly in a growable buffer. All complete frames are parsed synchronously in the data callback.
3. **Handshake:**
   - send the ClientGreeting (token from `authenticate()`, which may be async);
   - validate the ServerGreeting;
   - apply the peer limits (`MaxMessageSize` is the min of both; a value below 65,526 is a ProtocolError);
   - read `idleTimeoutMs` (S04).
4. **Disconnect handling.** Codes 20–38 are checked first; unknown codes are mapped; `DisconnectDelay` applies on send.
5. **Write path.** A queue flushed per microtask (one transport write per tick) that respects transport backpressure.
6. **Control sender.** Coalesces pong, window updates, credit and aborts.
7. **Timers.** Handshake, connection and progress timeouts, and the idle ping (interval from the advertised timeout). In the browser, run the ping in a dedicated Worker when available, falling back to `setInterval`. Use the `performance.now()` clock, with an **injectable clock** for tests.
8. **State machine.** `NexusClient` states and events (`EventTarget`): `statechange` and `disconnected`. Never resolve a user promise re-entrantly.
9. **Plaintext warning (D7)** and `isSecure`.
10. **Diagnostic counters** (for the H05 leak audit): open sessions, timers, pending writes.
