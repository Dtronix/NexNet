## Steps
1. **Pipe manager.**
   - Local IDs 1..255; 0 means unassigned.
   - **Both roles:**
     - **Opener:** the client opens a pipe via a proxy method sent with `IgnoreReturn|DuplexPipe`.
     - **Registrant:** the server opens a pipe to the client via `context.createPipe`. The client sends Ready with its window, and the opener sends `PipeWindowUpdate` after Ready.
   - `MaxPipes`, the session budget, refusal (`NexusBusyError`), half-close, `ready` and `completed` promises, and stall detection.
2. **`NexusDuplexPipe`.**
   - `readable`: `ReadableStream({type:'bytes'})` with `highWaterMark: 0`. Data is delivered in `pull()`. Count consumed bytes and send `PipeWindowUpdate` once at least half the window has been consumed since the last grant. **Grant only on consumption.**
   - `writable`: `WritableStream` with `highWaterMark: 0`. Split chunks at the credit boundary and await credit.
3. **Relaying.** `pipe.forwardTo(other)` / `bridge(a, b)`: half-close the destination and never complete the source.
4. **Channels.**
   - Varint length-prefix framing over a pipe.
   - `NexusChannelWriter<T>`: `write`, `writeMany` (one flush), `complete`, `writeAndComplete`.
   - `NexusChannelReader<T>`: `[Symbol.asyncIterator]` and `read()`, `readBatch(max)`, `readUntilComplete`.
   - Extra-credit rule for items larger than the window (`flow-control.md` §12): when waiting on an incomplete item whose remaining bytes exceed the writer's credit, grant all consumed credit at once.
   - Pipe reset on a bad item.
5. **Counters.** Open pipes and buffered bytes.

## Verify
The JS gate, including a property test: random chunk sizes, windows and item sizes; no stall; the byte stream is preserved.
