## Steps
1. **Credit gate.** A FIFO `CreditGate`: an `ArrayDeque` of waiting futures under a lock.
2. **Outbound calls.** As in J07:
   - results and remote error detail map to `NexusRemoteInvocationException`;
   - unauthorized maps to `NexusUnauthorizedException`;
   - large messages.
3. **Cancellation.**
   - `CancellationToken` registrations are closed when the call completes.
   - A cancel before send completes exceptionally with `CancellationException` (D17).
   - `future.cancel(true)` sends `InvocationCancellation`.
4. **Inbound calls.**
   - A bounded executor per session: a `Semaphore` over the shared invocation pool.
   - A separate pool for `SeparatePool` methods.
   - Method-ID validation.
   - Invocation credit is returned at dequeue for execution.
   - Results, including streamed results.
   - The inbound cancellation token.
5. **Auth.** `authenticate` accepts a synchronous or asynchronous supplier.
