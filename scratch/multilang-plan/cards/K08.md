## API shape
- **`NexusDuplexPipe`:**
  - `CompletableFuture<ByteBuffer> read()` (null at completion);
  - `CompletableFuture<Void> write(ByteBuffer)`, which completes when the credit is consumed;
  - `completeOutput()`, `ready()`, `completed()`;
  - `InputStream inputStream()` and `OutputStream outputStream()` blocking adapters, documented as "never on the Android main thread";
  - `forwardTo` and `bridge`.
- **`NexusChannelReader<T>`:** `CompletableFuture<Optional<T>> read()`, `readBatch(int max)` and `readUntilComplete()`.
- **`NexusChannelWriter<T>`:** `write(T)`, `writeAll(Iterable<T>)` (one flush), `complete()` and `writeAndComplete`.

## Rules
- Both pipe roles (opener and registrant).
- Credit is granted only on consumption.
- The extra-credit rule for oversized items.
- Reset on a bad item.
- Counters for the leak audit.
