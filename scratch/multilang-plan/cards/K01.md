## The contract (skeleton; mirrors J01)
1. **Codec:** `MsgPackWriter`, `MsgPackReader`, `Formatter<T>` (`write(MsgPackWriter, T)`, `T read(MsgPackReader)`), `FormatterRegistry`.
2. **Value types** in `com.dtronix.nexnet.values`: `NexusDecimal` (with a `BigDecimal` converter), `NexusDateTime`, `NexusDateTimeOffset`, `NexusTimeSpan`, `NexusDateOnly`, `NexusTimeOnly`, `NexusVersion`, `Tuple1`..`Tuple7`, `UnsignedLongs` helpers.
3. **Client:**
   - `NexusClient<TProxy>` with `connect()`, `tryConnect()`, `disconnect()`, `state()`, `addStateListener`, `disconnected()`, `proxy()`, `isSecure()`, `close()`.
   - `ClientConfig` builder with the transports `webSocket(uri)`, `httpSocket(uri)`, `tcp(host, port)` and `tcpTls(host, port)`, and options for:
     - TLS: `SSLContext`, trust/pin, client key, hostname verification on by default;
     - `authenticate` as a `Supplier<byte[]>` or `Supplier<CompletableFuture<byte[]>>`;
     - `callbackExecutor`;
     - `invocationExecutor`.
4. **Generated-code SPI:**
   - `ProxyBase` with `invoke(methodId, args, Formatter<R>, CancellationToken)`, `invokeVoid`, `invokePipe` and `invokeChannel`;
   - `ClientNexusBase` with `onConnected(boolean)`, `onDisconnected`, `onReconnecting`, `context()` and `dispatch(int methodId, MsgPackReader, InvocationContext)`;
   - `HandshakeDescriptor`.
5. **Pipes, channels, collections and pool:** `NexusDuplexPipe`, `NexusChannelReader<T>`/`Writer<T>`, `NexusList<T>`, `NexusClientPool`.
6. **Cancellation:** `CancellationToken` and `CancellationTokenSource`, with closeable registrations.
7. **Exceptions:** `NexusTransportException`, `NexusRemoteInvocationException`, `NexusUnauthorizedException`, `NexusBusyException`, `NexusMessageTooLargeException`.
